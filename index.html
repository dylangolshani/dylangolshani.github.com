What is the Japanese Domestic Market?

The Japanese domestic market refers to vehicles or vehicle parts which originate from Japan. Cars designed, built, and sold in Japan are labeled as JDM. USDM (US Domestic Market) is the same but for items orginating from the states. Some examples of Japanse car companies include:

Japanese Car Culture

Japanese car culture is both expressive and diverse. Cars are modified in many different ways making each car unique. Modifcations can be done to the engine, the car body, and intertior. These are just a few. As the market fluctuates, people like to show off their bone stock cars. Bone stock is a term referring to unmodified vehicles that have no aftermarket parts. Vintage cars that are stock retain significant value as older clean cars are difficult to find. Additionally, cars can be tuned for specific competitions like drifting, drag racing, the track, or even street racing. A famous car meet location in Japan is Daikoku PA (Parking Area); here, people show off their cars for others to admire.
